## Limits of this verification

- No gong recording was supplied, so playback intentionally does nothing. Audible playback, focus/DND behavior, alarm volume and recording decay require device testing after adding a licensed OGG/WAV file. No claim is made that a gong was heard.
- Normal completion, silent/voiced boundary event eligibility, continuous rollover and long-term drift were tested deterministically rather than waiting through real meditation intervals. The physical feel of boundary vibration was not assessed.
- The current visuals are replaceable procedural/vector fallback artwork, not final painted assets.
- Android versions other than 13, TalkBack, large font configurations, extended Doze/vendor battery restrictions and overnight operation still need manual coverage. The short screen-off check is not an overnight battery/reliability test.
- Reboot continuation is intentionally unsupported. Missed sounds are never replayed after process restoration.
